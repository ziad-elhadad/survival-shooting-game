Instructions

How to Play:

  • WASD or Arrow Keys - Move player
  • Mouse - Aim
  • Left Click or Space - Shoot
  • R - Reload
  • P or ESC - Pause

Objective:

Survive waves of enemies by shooting them down. Each wave increases in difficulty. Collect power-ups to improve your chances. Don't let your health reach zero!
